# Shadow Money Wizards — Card List

**Identity:** shady wizards who deal in money and magic. Big, impressive spells. Wizard creatures that bring **value**, not finishers. **Shady deals**: trade life or cards for Gold, or give opponents Gold in exchange for a big effect.
**Faction pie:** card draw ★, Gold ★, removal through bounce / bribe / counter, keyword Flying. No healing, no big creatures, no graveyard play.

**Target:** 20 cards, 9 Common / 6 Uncommon / 4 Rare / 1 Legendary.
Curve target: about 3 one-drops, 4 two-drops, 4 three-drops, 3 four-drops, 3 five-drops, 2 six-drops, and 1 card at 7+.

Status tags: ✅ approved · 🟡 draft · ✏️ needs changes · ❌ cut
