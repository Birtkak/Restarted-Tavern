# Sensationalists — Card List

**Identity:** occult humans (MTG black + LoR Shadow Isles). Cults, séances and mediums who turn death into a public **spectacle**: a horror show broadcast for an audience.
**Faction pie:** graveyard ★. Removal through destroy, sacrifice and -X/-X. Pay life to draw. Healing through drain and Lifelink. Tokens from deaths. **Curses** are the core card type. Keywords: Flying, Lifelink. A few big creatures, always with drawbacks. No Gold, no buffs.
**Role with permanent damage:** they don't care about damage. They profit when things **die**.

**Token:** *Spirit*, a 1/1 Creature: Spirit with Flying.

**Target:** 20 cards, 9 Common / 6 Uncommon / 4 Rare / 1 Legendary. Curve 3 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 2 / 1.
